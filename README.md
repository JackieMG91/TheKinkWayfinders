# TheKinkWayfinders
This is an app to help people learn about kink, to build strong kink communities, and have fun while doing it.

## Kinky Wayfinders — Ranking & Badge System

A two-track advancement system: **Ranks** (linear, community-and-knowledge based) and **Specialty Badges** (non-linear, interest-based). This mirrors traditional Scouting-style advancement systems: ranks measure well-roundedness and community involvement; badges measure depth in a specific area.

---

## Design Principles

- **Knowledge and character over acts.** Advancement is earned through education, communication skill, safety literacy, and community contribution — never through disclosing or performing specific sexual activities. Nobody should have to "prove" a kink to earn a badge.
- **Time-in-rank + mentor sign-off** prevents rank from being purchasable or rushable, and builds real mentorship relationships.
- **Every rank includes a safety/health component.** No skipping it, no matter how experienced someone claims to be.
- **Self-paced, opt-in.** Ranks are entirely optional flavor for people who want structure and recognition — never gatekeeping for basic app access.

### Pre-Rank — Applicant
*Everyone starts here immediately after sign-up + age/identity verification. Not yet a ranked member.*
- Complete profile (boundaries, identity, pronouns, discretion settings)
- Read & accept Community Guidelines and Code of Conduct
- Complete "Welcome to the Crew" orientation module (what this app is for, how reporting works, how consent culture works here)
- **Swear the Kinky Wayfinders Oath in front of the Crew** (live at a meeting/event, or via a live virtual gathering — not a solo checkbox)

> **The Kinky Wayfinders Oath**
> *"I give myself to this Crew, and pledge to follow the laws of the Wayfinders and the laws of my Crew.*
> *I speak this oath freely — with an open mind, an open heart, and under no pressure from anyone.*
> *I will not judge my fellow Wayfinders for whatever kink or fetish they engage in. It's not my cup of tea, but it is their cup of tea.*
> *I promise to seek knowledge before action, and consent before all.*
> *I will speak my limits honestly, and honor the limits of those I play with.*
> *I will care for my community as I want my community to care for me.*
> *I will make room for every body, mind, gender, identity, creed, and race.*
> *Above all — I will play safe, play smart, and play kind.*
> *I will respect those who walk the path above me, and when my time comes, guide those who walk behind.*
> *For in our own way, we are all submissive to something — to our limits, to our community, and to the trust we place in each other.*
> *This I swear, before this Crew and by my own word."*

**Swearing the oath is what grants Initiate rank.** It's the on-ramp ritual — the moment an Applicant stops being a solo account and becomes a witnessed, recognized member of a Crew. Regional/online Crews without in-person meetings can do this via a scheduled live video gathering; it shouldn't be satisfied by a text-only checkbox, since the point is the shared, witnessed moment.

### Rank 1 — Initiate
*Granted the moment the Oath is sworn in front of the Crew.*
- Officially welcomed and recognized as a Crew member
- Gains access to Crew discussion boards, event listings, and the badge/rank progression system

### Rank 2 — Disciple
**Requirements (complete all):**
- 🎖 Badge: *Consent Fundamentals*
- 🎖 Badge: *Safety & Risk-Awareness Basics*
- Join at least 1 Crew (community group)
- Attend or watch 1 intro event/AMA (in-person or virtual)

### Rank 3 — Apprentice
**Requirements:**
- 🎖 Badge: *Communication & Negotiation*
- 🎖 Badge: *Sexual Health Literacy* (testing, barrier methods, disclosure conversations)
- Post or contribute meaningfully to 3 community discussions
- Attend 2 events/munches

**Unlocks:** Assistant-mentor eligibility — can be paired with a Seafarer as a co-mentor/second set of hands for an Initiate or Disciple (e.g., helping run a badge session, covering event buddy-up duties). Apprentices don't mentor solo and can't sign off on badge completion themselves; the Seafarer they're paired with remains accountable for the mentee's progress.

### Rank 4 — Seafarer
**Requirements:**
- 🎖 2 Specialty Badges of the member's choosing (see below)
- 🎖 Badge: *Aftercare & Emotional Regulation*
- Attend 4 events OR help organize 1
- Give a peer a positive endorsement (light peer-review system)

**Unlocks:** Full mentorship eligibility — can be matched with Initiates/Disciples for guidance and event buddy-up, and can bring on an Apprentice as an assistant-mentor to help.

### Rank 5 — Navigator
**Requirements:**
- 🎖 3 additional Specialty Badges (5 total)
- 🎖 Badge: *Recognizing & Responding to Red Flags* (coercion, grooming, community safety)
- Co-host or organize 1 event
- **Master a mentee all the way to Apprentice rank** (required, not optional — assisting as an Apprentice doesn't count toward this; the mentor must have been lead throughout, and the mentee must complete Initiate → Disciple → Apprentice under their guidance). **If a mentorship ends early** (ghosting, mismatch, mentee going inactive), progress **resets** — the mentor must guide a new mentee through the full arc from the start. No partial credit carries over between mentees.
- Mentor-nomination: an existing Master+ vouches for their conduct

**Unlocks:** Crew leadership track — voting rights on Crew-level decisions (local badge proposals, event calendar, Crew guidelines within global rules).

### Rank 6 — Master
**Requirements:**
- Complete *Mentor Certification* module (how to teach, how to support without overstepping)
- Have mentored 2 additional people since reaching Navigator (3 total career mentees, building on the Navigator requirement)
- Clean conduct record (no unresolved reports)
- Approved by a Grand Master-rank panel or app moderators

**Unlocks:** Formal mentorship sign-off authority — can officially certify a mentee's hands-on badge requirements (e.g., confirming a rope safety demonstration was completed correctly).

### Rank 7 — Grand Master
- Capped number per Crew/region — this is a stewardship role, not a "final boss" flex
- Nominated by Masters, confirmed by community vote + moderator vetting
- Responsibilities: conflict mediation support, event vetting, onboarding new Masters
- Comes with real moderation-adjacent permissions, so requires the strongest vetting in the app

### Rank 8 — Sage
- Reached only after significant time *at* Grand Master rank (not skippable) — this is an emeritus/wisdom-keeper role, not "Grand Master but more powerful"
- Nominated by current Sages + Grand Master vote; no self-nomination
- **Role shifts from active governance to institutional memory and mentorship of Grand Master-rank members themselves** — Sages mentor the mentors
- Sits on an advisory council staff can consult for community history/precedent (e.g., "how has this kind of dispute been handled before"), but **holds no more unilateral power than Grand Master** — deliberately not a "final boss" tier of authority, to avoid the exact power-concentration problem the Grand Master power model was built to prevent
- Often the people who help design or revise Crew-specific traditions, welcome new Crews into the network, or represent the community at a cross-Crew level
- Optional real-world touch: Sages could be the ones who *administer* the Oath to new Applicants at bigger/flagship Crew events, tying the rank back to the ritual that started everyone's journey

## Earning a Badge — Standard Flow
1. **Learn** — short in-app module (article, video, or podcast-style audio)
2. **Reflect** — a written prompt or short quiz (not gatekept behind a "perfect score," just genuine engagement)
3. **Apply** *(optional, for hands-on badges)* — a mentor or event host can sign off that the member demonstrated the skill/knowledge appropriately (e.g., a rope safety badge might involve a mentor confirming the member correctly identified nerve-danger zones on a diagram, not any actual play)
4. **Badge awarded** — appears on profile, contributes toward rank progress

## Renewal & Expiry System

Not everything expires — only the badges/ranks where *outdated* knowledge is actually risky. A stale "Communication 101" badge isn't dangerous; a 3-year-old idea of "current STI testing practices" or "safeword protocols" can be.

**Tier 1 — Expires (18-month cycle, renewal = short refresher quiz, not full redo):**
- Sexual Health Literacy
- Risk-Awareness & Harm Reduction
- Recognizing & Responding to Red Flags
- Rope Safety & Anatomy Awareness
- Impact Play Safety & Body Mechanics
- Consent Fundamentals *(evolving best practices)*

**Tier 2 — Never expires (foundational/reflective, not fact-dependent):**
- Communication & Negotiation
- Identity & Inclusion badges
- Aftercare & Emotional Regulation
- Community & Leadership badges

**Rank-level renewal:**
- Ranks themselves don't expire, **except** that any rank requiring a Tier 1 badge shows a "renewal due" flag on the profile until the underlying badge is refreshed. A lapsed safety badge doesn't demote someone, but it visibly pauses their eligibility to mentor or host events until renewed — so the system nudges via privileges, not public shame.
- Master/Grand Master specifically require an annual "still in good standing" check (conduct record review), separate from badge content.

**Grace period before privilege pause:**
- First renewal reminder fires **30 days before** expiry.
- A follow-up reminder fires **7 days before** expiry.
- At expiry, a **2-week grace period** begins — the badge shows "expired, renew now" but mentorship/hosting privileges stay active.
- Only if the grace period lapses without renewal does the privilege pause take effect. This ensures nobody gets blindsided mid-mentorship or mid-event-planning by a sudden lockout — the system always gives clear advance warning plus a buffer before anything actually changes.

**UX note:** Show expiring badges with a countdown ("Renew by [date]") and an easy in-app renewal flow — friction should be minimal, this is about keeping knowledge current, not punishing people.

## Local Crew Badges

Regional/interest-based Crews can create their own custom badges layered on top of the global set.

**Membership gating:** Crews *can* require specific badges (global or local) to join — e.g., a rope-focused Crew requiring the global *Rope Safety & Anatomy Awareness* badge, or a munch-hosting Crew requiring *Event Planning & Venue Safety*. This isn't the default: Crews are open-access unless a Crew Lead (Navigator+, given the new voting-rights unlock) explicitly sets a gate, and any gate must be badge-based (demonstrated knowledge) rather than rank-based, so it stays about relevant competency rather than gatekeeping by seniority. Gated Crews should be labeled clearly in Crew search/browse ("Requires: Rope Safety badge") so people aren't surprised by a locked join button.

**Waivers:** A Crew Lead *can* waive a badge-gate for a specific applicant at their discretion (e.g., someone with real-world experience but no in-app badge yet, or a trusted transfer from another Crew). Waivers are logged and visible to that Crew's leadership (not public) so the exception is traceable rather than a quiet backdoor — keeps discretion available without it becoming an unaccountable loophole. **The system itself stays quiet about it to the applicant** (no "you were waived in" notification) so it doesn't read as a lesser form of membership — but if the applicant *chooses* to mention it themselves (e.g., "the Lead waived the badge requirement for me"), that's entirely fine and carries no penalty or standing consequence. The discretion is the Crew Lead's private call to make quietly; it's never a secret the member is obligated to keep.

- **Crew Leads (Master+) can propose a local badge**: name, description, learning module or requirement, icon.
- **Grand Master-rank review** before it goes live (see governance below) — keeps quality and tone consistent without making it purely top-down.
- Local badges are **flagged distinctly** on profiles (e.g., a small Crew icon overlay) so it's clear they're community-specific, not globally standardized/verified content.
- Local badges **never count toward global Rank progression** by default — they're flavor and local recognition. (Optional toggle: a Crew could designate 1–2 of its badges as "rank-eligible" with Grand Master approval, if you want to allow strong local programs to feed into the main track.)
- Prevents badge-name collisions: local badges get a namespaced tag (e.g., "Rope Safety — *Pacific NW Crew*") so they're never confused with the vetted global "Rope Safety & Anatomy Awareness" badge.

## Grand Master & Sage: Power Model

Real influence, not unilateral authority — deliberately designed against the "kink elder becomes untouchable" failure mode seen in a lot of real-world communities. **Sage shares this exact same ceiling; it is not a more powerful tier, just a more experienced/advisory one.**

**Grand Master & Sage CAN:**
- Flag users/content for priority review by paid trust & safety staff
- Approve or reject local Crew-proposed badges
- Vote on Master→Grand Master and Grand Master→Sage nominations
- Mediate disputes when both parties opt in
- Sit on the review panel that promotes members to Master rank
- *(Sage only)* Advise staff on precedent/community history when consulted

**Grand Master & Sage CANNOT:**
- Unilaterally ban, mute, suspend, or remove a user
- Access private reports/DMs beyond what any member can see
- Override staff moderation decisions

**Accountability:** All Grand Master and Sage actions are logged and staff-auditable — same transparency standard as any mod tool, which matters *more* here since both ranks are peer-elected rather than employment-vetted and background-checked staff.

## Mentorship & Leadership Unlocks

Mentorship and leadership are rank privileges, not badges — and now form a required pipeline rather than an optional add-on.

- **Apprentice (Rank 3)** unlocks *assistant*-mentor eligibility — can be paired with a Seafarer to help mentor an Initiate/Disciple (co-running sessions, event buddy-up support), but doesn't mentor solo or sign off on badges. Gets people a taste of mentoring a rank early, and gives Seafarers help with the workload.
- **Seafarer (Rank 4)** unlocks full mentorship eligibility — can be matched with Initiates/Disciples for guidance and event buddy-up, and can bring on an Apprentice as an assistant. This is where people start leading mentorship, not just assisting with it.
- **Navigator (Rank 5)** *requires* having guided a mentee all the way from Initiate to Apprentice rank as lead mentor — assisting as an Apprentice doesn't satisfy this. This is a meaningfully longer commitment than one badge; Navigator confirms someone can shepherd a full early-journey arc, not just tutor a single lesson. Reaching Navigator also unlocks **Crew leadership**: voting rights on Crew-level decisions like local badge approval, event calendar, and Crew-specific guidelines (within global rules).
- **Master (Rank 6)** unlocks *formal* sign-off authority — can officially certify a mentee's hands-on badge requirements — and requires 2 more mentees beyond the Navigator requirement (3 career mentees total, as lead mentor).
- Mentorship and voting eligibility are **paused** (not revoked) if a member has an unresolved report or a lapsed Tier 1 safety badge — same "privilege pause, not public shame" pattern as renewal.
- Mentee feedback visibility: visible to the mentor themselves, plus anyone ranked above the mentor (e.g., a Seafarer's feedback is visible to Navigator, Master, Grand Master, and Sage members, plus staff) — not visible to peers at the same rank or below. This gives leadership real signal on mentor quality when reviewing promotions (e.g., a Master-panel review of a Navigator candidate) without turning it into a public rating system. **The mentee is told exactly who can see their feedback** before submitting it (e.g., "Visible to: your mentor, all Navigator+ members, and staff") — full transparency, no fine print.

**Mentor matching — mutual choice, no algorithm:**
- Mentees browse available mentors' profiles (badges, specialties, Crew, a short bio/style blurb) and send a mentorship request to whoever they'd like.
- Mentors browse mentee requests (and can also browse newer members directly) and choose who to accept — mentors are never auto-assigned a mentee they didn't agree to.
- A match only forms when **both sides opt in** — either party can decline without needing to give a reason, and declining doesn't penalize either person's standing.
- Given the real stakes now attached to mentorship (a Navigator candidate's whole requirement rides on one mentee relationship), mutual choice matters more than throughput — a bad forced match would be worse than a slower, chosen one.
- If a match doesn't work out mid-way, either party can end it respectfully; the mentor can then seek a new mentee to fulfill their Navigator requirement rather than being stuck.
- **Request caps are a Crew-level decision, not a global rule.** Individual Crew Leads can set a max number of open mentee requests a mentor can hold at once, if they see a bottleneck forming around popular mentors — but there's no platform-wide limit, since Crew size and mentor availability vary a lot. **Confirmed fully hands-off:** no global floor or ceiling on Crew-set caps — Crews are trusted to calibrate this for their own size and culture.
- **Soft support after an early end:** when a mentorship ends before completion, the mentor gets a **"Back on the Market" flag** — a temporary boost that surfaces them higher in mentee browse/search for a limited window (e.g., 2 weeks), so restarting doesn't mean quietly waiting to be noticed again. This is opt-in (the mentor can dismiss it if they want a break) and carries no visible "this mentorship failed" marker — mentees just see an available mentor, not a history of what happened. The point is cushioning the reset, not flagging what went wrong.

## Equivalency & Prior Experience

For people who bring real years of experience from other communities, scenes, or organizations before ever joining Wayfinders.

**Core principle:** Outside experience can earn *credit toward specific requirements* — it should never buy a rank outright. The Oath, mentorship (given and received), and community-specific trust always have to be built fresh inside Wayfinders, since those are the things that make rank actually mean something here rather than just being a badge count.

### The Equivalency Review

- An applicant submits a **portfolio**: years active, communities/organizations they've been part of, roles held (event organizer, educator, DM/dungeon monitor, etc.), and 2–3 references who can vouch for them.
- Reviewed by a small panel of **Master+ members** — the same rank tier that already holds formal sign-off authority in the badge system.
- **What the panel CAN grant:**
  - Credit toward specific **knowledge badges** via a "challenge" process — demonstrating competency directly to the panel instead of completing the intro module (e.g., someone who's taught rope safety for a decade can challenge the Rope Safety badge rather than sit through it from scratch)
  - **Reduced time-in-rank** for the lower ranks (Initiate → Disciple → Apprentice can move faster with verified outside experience)
- **What the panel CANNOT grant:**
  - The Oath itself — always sworn fresh, in front of the actual Crew. This is the community-joining ritual, not a knowledge checkpoint, and it can't be waived by credential.
  - Mentorship requirements — Navigator's requirement to guide someone through Wayfinders specifically doesn't transfer from outside mentoring, because it's *this* community's trust being built, not a general mentoring skill.
  - **Any rank above Seafarer on entry, full stop** — regardless of outside experience, Navigator and above require having actually led and mentored inside Wayfinders. A 15-year veteran of another scene can still be new to how *this* specific mentorship pipeline and community actually function.

### Recognition without rank inflation

Give experienced newcomers a visible marker of their outside history that isn't rank itself: a **"Veteran" flair/badge** displayed alongside their current rank pin. This solves the real thing experienced people are worried about — not wanting to look like a total beginner — without letting outside credentials quietly inflate what a Wayfinders rank actually certifies.

### Why the entry cap matters

If equivalency review could place someone straight at Navigator or Master, it would undercut the exact thing the mentorship pipeline was built to prove: that someone can shepherd *this* community's newcomers through *this* specific system. That's unavoidably a first-hand thing to demonstrate, no matter how deep someone's outside resume runs.
